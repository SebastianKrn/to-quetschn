# Takedown Policy (Draft)

Status: Draft - not approved

## Intake
- Accept takedown requests via designated support/legal channel.
- Log request timestamp, claimant identity, claimed work, and evidence.

## Temporary Action
- Hide reported public arrangement immediately while review is in progress.

## Review
- Validate claimant rights and linkage to reported content.
- Decide retain/remove/escalate.

## Outcome
- Notify claimant and uploader of decision.
- Keep immutable audit record of decisions and timestamps.
