# Sharing Policy (Current)

Status: Active default policy

## Default Rule
- Public sharing is disabled by default (`FEATURE_PUBLIC_SHARING=false`).
- Users may keep arrangements private in their own workspace/library.

## Rationale
Legal handling for copyrighted works is not finalized.

## Required Before Enabling Public Sharing
1. Terms of use finalized
2. Takedown workflow implemented and reviewed
3. Rights/licensing checks documented
4. Explicit product flag approval by owner
